# Links between plans

Plans have started pointing at each other. Tallow revisits pumice (bodkin's
`revisits:`), and entries anchor to other plans' entries: three in mock-control at
`f02528f`, seven in aide at `248a2a5`. Phoropter treats each of those as an anchor into
a markdown file and says "no quote, so nothing to search for," while the entry it
means already has a page address. This plan makes them links.

What an agent can query without reading prose (sources, verdicts, dates, and the rest
from Jeff's daily in this cart) is the next cart's question, not this one's.

## Decision: `revisits:` is a `revisits` link, and the revisited plan shows `revisited-by`
- where: `src/render.ts`
- quote: `${frontmatterRest(plan.doc.frontmatter, ['cart', 'base'])}`

**Because (bodkin, Jeff):** bodkin names the rels and says the old plan shows
`revisited-by` without being edited, which a generated page gets for free. Both go in
the plan's facts list, and the home page says "revisits pumice" beside tallow. A
`revisits:` naming a plan this site doesn't have is shown as written, with a plain
note.

## Decision: An anchor at `plans/<file>.md › <entry title>` resolves to that entry
- where: `src/resolve.ts`
- quote: `const [file, ...path] = where.split(' › ');`

**Because (measured):** all ten such anchors in mock-control and aide match their target
entry's title exactly, backticks included. Matching is exact, the same as an answer
and its guess. If no entry in that plan or as-built has the title, the page says "no
entry titled this in pumice." Anchors that name entries don't make a file page for the
plan's markdown.

## Decision: These anchors reuse `anchored-at` and `anchored-by`
- where: `src/render.ts`
- quote: `['anchored-at', 'phoropter', 'Where in the code does this entry point? From an anchor on an entry to the page for its symbol or file.'],`

**Because (Claude, agreed by Jeff):** where an entry points, and who points here, is one
question whatever is at the far end. The plans already call these anchors, and the
target page says what it is through its microdata. The profile says `anchored-at` can
lead to an entry as well as to a symbol or file.

## Decision: An entry anchor shows the target's kind and title, linked, not views at two commits
- where: `src/site.ts`
- quote: `ra.note = 'no quote, so nothing to search for';`

**Because (Jeff, and aide's awl):** a plan is frozen when committed, and an as-built
when its work lands, so both ends of the anchor are frozen. Two views would show the
same text twice. It's resolved at the generated commit, and the anchor names the plan
it points into.

## Decision: An entry anchor with a quote also shows the entry's text, the quote highlighted
**Because (Claude's fallback, agreed by Jeff):** grommet anchors lanyard's entry "What
changed during the work goes in `<plan>.as-built.md`, beside it" with the quote
``Its entries are `Changed:`.``, which says which sentence it meant. It's one view, at
the generated commit, and the search stays inside the target entry, as gimlet keeps a
quote inside its anchor's file. If the quote isn't in the entry, it says so.

## Decision: The target entry lists what anchors it, under its heading
**Because (Claude, unanswered, so its fallback):** the same place and shape as "Answered
in the as-built": "Anchored by", then each anchoring entry, linked, with its plan's
name.

## Decision: An anchor at a whole plan file links to the plan's page, and keeps its quote search
**Because (Claude, unanswered, so its fallback):** a quote into a plan's frontmatter is
evidence like any other. Untested: the only one, bodkin's anchor at tallow, is
cross-repo from aide.

## Decline: A transitive link from pumice's guess straight to vellum's answer
**Because (Claude, unanswered, so its fallback):** quince declined this as the cart's
question, and two hops now cover it. Vellum's guess anchors pumice's, and is answered
in vellum's as-built. A link that skips a hop would say vellum answered pumice's guess,
which only vellum's prose says.

## Decline: Sources, verdicts, dates, orientation, absolute URLs, and cross-plan pages
**Because (Jeff and Claude):** Jeff raised them in this cart, and we agreed on most of
how. They're about what an agent can query, not links between plans, and a cart that
wants two plans splits. They go to the shelf at conversion.

## Guess: Exact entry titles are a stable enough key for anchors between plans
**Disproved if:** an anchor fails to match an entry it plainly means, through a typo, a
rephrase, or backticks written differently. Plans are frozen, so a title can't drift
after the fact; a miss can only come from how the anchor was written.
