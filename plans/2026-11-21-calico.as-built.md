# As built: calico

Built as planned. `go test -count=1 ./...` passes, and `GOOS=windows go vet` does.
Phoropter's own home page went from 55 KB to 21 KB: fourteen recent plans (the 19th to
the 21st) and 26 open guesses. The full lists are `plans/` (32 KB), `files/` (19 KB)
and `symbols/` (7 KB).

## Changed: The place door says "each file plans point at", not "any file's page"
- where: `internal/site/render.go`
- quote: `<li>From a place: <code>phoropter affected &lt;file&gt;</code>`

**Because (Claude, not asked):** the plan said any file's page is at `files/<path>/`,
which isn't true: only files a plan anchors have a page. `affected` answers for any
file; the page exists only where there's something to list.

## Chose: Open guesses are `openGuess` items, with `url`, `title` and `plan`
- where: `internal/site/profile.go`
- quote: ``{`openGuess`, `On the home page: a guess still open``

**Because (Claude, not asked):** the plan named the list but not its microdata. An
`entry` item would promise a reason, anchors and the rest, which a line in a list
doesn't carry, so it's a property of its own, and the profile says what it holds.

## Chose: The golden comparison checks the old home page's items against the three lists
- where: `internal/site/golden_test.go`
- quote: `func homeMoved(t *testing.T, golden, got map[string]string) {`

**Because (Claude, not asked):** the Node version's home page is the record of what the
full listing held. Each of its `plan` and `place` items, flattened, must be on exactly
one of `plans/`, `files/` or `symbols/` and nothing else must be, on all three golden
sites; the served one checks `noteCount` that way too. Then both home pages and the
lists leave the page-by-page comparison, which is the deliberate change.

## Chose: The note count is on `plans/` too
- where: `internal/site/serve.go`
- quote: `if here == "" || here == "plans/" {`

**Because (Claude, not asked):** `noteCount` was on every plan in the home page's list.
Recent plans keep it, and the full list has it, so a plan with notes says so wherever
it's listed, and the TUI, which reads `plans/`, keeps its counts.

## Chose: Two of the TUI's test sites stay in the old form
- where: `tui/README.md`
- quote: ``no `index` links.``

**Because (Claude, not asked):** `mock-control-f02528f/` and `phoropter-1a83dbc/` are
rebuilt and read through the `index` links. `mock-control-draft/` and
`phoropter-lenses/` are captured served pages from before this plan, so they test the
fallback: a home page with no `index` links is the whole list, as a site built before
calico, or published from an older phoropter, still is.
