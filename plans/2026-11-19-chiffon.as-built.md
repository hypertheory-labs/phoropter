## Chose: The port check dials 127.0.0.1, not the record's host
- where: `main.go`
- quote: `c, err := net.DialTimeout("tcp", "127.0.0.1:"+u.Port(), time.Second)`

**Because (Claude, not asked):** the record says `localhost`, and on Windows
`localhost` can resolve to `::1` first, while the server listens on `127.0.0.1` only.
Dialing what the server actually listens on avoids a slow or failed first try.
