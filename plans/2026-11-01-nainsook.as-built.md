## Chose: A place's reference is what an anchor at it says
- where: `tui/app/links.go`
- quote: `// A place's reference is what an anchor at it says: its symbol, or its file.`

**Because (Claude, not asked):** the plan only named entries. On a place's page, the
durable thing to hand someone is the symbol or path a plan's `where:` would use. It's
also what a lens holding places would contain, which `shelf/lenses.md` leaves open.

## Chose: `Y` on the plan list says to open the plan
- where: `tui/app/links.go`
- quote: `// The home page doesn't say a plan's file, and the TUI doesn't guess it`

**Because (Claude, not asked):** the home page lists each plan's name and date, but not its
file. The TUI could build `plans/<date>-<name>.md` from them, but that's the format's
rule computed outside phoropter. The boundary says the TUI computes only what it can
see. If `Y` on the list turns out to matter, the fix is to put `file` on the home page's
plans, which is phoropter's change to make.

## Chose: On a site on disk, a directory's address gets `index.html`
- where: `tui/app/links.go`
- quote: `// linkable is an address a browser opens as the page: a site on disk serves`

**Because (Claude, not asked):** a `file://` directory opens as a folder listing, not the
page, so `b` on a built site would have shown the wrong thing. A served site is
unchanged.
