# As built: dimity

Nothing departed from the plan, so there's no `Changed:`. The experiment's agent read
the answers (`ANSWERS.md`, in its repository, written in this session at Jeff's request)
and acted on two of them while this was built:
- the roslyn indexer names packages by `AssemblyName` now;
- the scip-dotnet wrapper no longer restores by default.

It measured the second: offline with an empty cache, the wrapper now finishes in 52s with
249 of 251 definitions, the Aspire host project in `missing`, where before it failed
after two minutes. That 52s is MSBuild waiting out an SDK download. The 5-minute limit
leaves room for it. Two minutes would have cut that commit to `no-index`.
