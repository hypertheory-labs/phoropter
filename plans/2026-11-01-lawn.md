# Lenses take notes: nested under an item, and written in your editor

Jeff sees lenses as prompt prep: collect entries, then write around them what you want
to talk through. Percale left notes to the file, and the file doesn't take them well. A
nested bullet under an item becomes a new item that resolves to nothing, and a paragraph
between items is silently dropped. This makes the file take notes the way you'd naturally
write them, and gives the TUI a key to open it.

## Decision: An indented line under an item is that item's note
- where: ``scip-go gomod github.com/hypertheory-labs/phoropter . `github.com/hypertheory-labs/phoropter/internal/site`/ParseLens().``
- quote: `case strings.HasPrefix(t, "- ") || strings.HasPrefix(t, "* "):`

**Because (Jeff):** "maybe a one level nest on the bullets?" An indented line after an
item, whether a bullet or prose, belongs to that item. Its note is that block, rendered as
markdown, after any ` — note` on the item's own line. One level is all it reads: deeper
indentation is just part of the note.

## Decision: Prose after the list belongs to the lens, and nothing is dropped
- where: ``scip-go gomod github.com/hypertheory-labs/phoropter . `github.com/hypertheory-labs/phoropter/internal/site`/ParseLens().``
- quote: `case len(l.Items) == 0 && l.Title != "":`

**Because (Claude, not asked):** the lens page promises to show every line as written, and
today it drops unindented text between and after items. That text is the lens's own
closing note, shown after the list. A paragraph between items closes the item above it,
like any unindented line.

## Decision: `+` adds after the last item and its notes
- where: ``scip-go gomod github.com/hypertheory-labs/phoropter . `github.com/hypertheory-labs/phoropter/tui/notes`/AppendLens().``
- quote: `return path, true, os.WriteFile(path, []byte(text+"- "+span+"\n"), 0o644)`

**Because (Claude, not asked):** appending at the end of the file would put a new item
below a lens's closing text, where it reads as part of the note. It goes right after the
last item's block instead. It still never changes a line already there.

## Decision: `e` on a lens opens it in your editor
- where (new): `tui/app/lens.go`

**Because (Jeff, then Claude):** collecting is `+`, and "later" is the intro, the notes,
reordering, and pruning: all editing, and a lens is plain markdown. `e` on a lens, or on
the lens list's chosen one, opens the file in `$VISUAL` or `$EDITOR`, as `ctrl+e` does in
a form. The lens reads again when you return. It needs the lens's repository on disk, so
it says so when there isn't one.

## Guess: A lens written as prompt prep reads back whole
**Disproved if:** a lens with an intro, nested notes under items, a paragraph between
items, and closing text shows any of that text missing, or any of it as an item, on the
lens page or in the TUI.
