# As built: sorrel

Nothing departed from the plan, so there's no `Changed:`. The code is one commit
rather than several, because revisits and entry anchors share the same restructuring
of `site.ts`.

## Chose: Every entry exists before any anchor resolves
- where: `src/site.ts`
- quote: `// Every entry exists before any anchor resolves, since an anchor can name`

**Because (Claude, not asked):** entries used to be built and resolved in one pass,
plan by plan. Tallow anchors pumice, which comes earlier, but tallow's as-built anchors
vellum's, which comes later, so one pass can't see it. Two passes: make every entry,
then resolve the anchors.

## Chose: An entry anchor's link is the `target` property, apart from `place`
- where: `src/render.ts`
- quote: `` ? `<p class="target"><span class="label">entry</span> <a rel="anchored-at" itemprop="target" href="${rel(here, ra.entry.doc.href)}#${ra.entry.entry.slug}">${kindTitle(ra.entry.entry)}</a> <span class="meta">in ${docName(ra.entry)}</span></p>` ``

**Because (Claude, not asked):** the rel is shared, as the plan says, but the microdata
property says what's at the far end. `place` is a symbol or file page and `target` is
another plan's entry, or a whole plan. An agent can follow `anchored-at` without
caring, or ask for one kind.

## Chose: The quoted entry is found by its heading, and shown whole
- where: `src/resolve.ts`
- quote: ``const heading = lines.findIndex((l) => /^## /.test(l) && l.replace(/^## (?:\w+: )?/, '') === title);``

**Because (Claude, not asked):** the plan said the search stays inside the entry. The
entry runs from its `## Kind: title` line to the next `##`, with trailing blank lines
dropped. It's shown whole, not as 3 lines of context, because an entry is short and its
heading tells the reader which entry this is. For grommet's quote of lanyard, that's
lines 34 to 40, with line 39 highlighted.

## Chose: A whole-plan anchor makes no back-link on the plan it names
**Because (Claude, not asked):** the plan gave entries an "Anchored by" line and said
nothing about whole plans. The only such anchor is bodkin's, which is cross-repo from
aide, so nothing here would exercise a back-link. It waits for an anchor that needs it.

## Chose: Code-span unwrapping stays lenient, pinned by a test
- where: `test/plan.test.ts`
- quote: `// Lenient on purpose: awl (aide 248a2a5) wraps a title that contains`

**Because (Claude, not asked, at conversion):** this was a write-ahead finding in the
cart, and conversion has to promote it or drop it. Awl is frozen, and its anchor at
grommet only means what its writer meant if the unwrapping is lenient. Strict
CommonMark would be more correct and would break a real anchor. How to write a title
that contains backticks is a format question, and it belongs to aide.

## Answered: Exact entry titles are a stable enough key for anchors between plans
**Answer (measured):** held, on ten of ten: three in mock-control at `f02528f`, seven
in aide at `248a2a5`. One of the ten matched only because phoropter's code-span
unwrapping is lenient. Awl's anchor at grommet wraps a title containing backticks in
single backticks, which CommonMark would split at the first inner backtick. That's the
"backticks written differently" case in this guess, caught by the reader rather than
the writer. If the unwrapping is ever made strict, that anchor stops matching.
