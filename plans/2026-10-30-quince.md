# Name plans by their file, and read grommet's kinds

Generating mock-control at `f02528f`, which has three new plans (vellum, ferrule,
tallow), showed two things phoropter gets wrong that don't need a cart. Plans
overwrite each other's pages, and as-builts in grommet's kinds (aide
`plans/2026-10-30-grommet.md`) render without a kind. Revisits (bodkin) and anchors
from one plan into another wait for a cart.

## Decision: A plan's page is named by its filename, not its cart
- where: `src/site.ts`
- quote: `` const href = `plans/${slugify(cart)}/`; ``

**Because (measured):** vellum, ferrule, and tallow all say `cart: none; Jeff handed
mock-control's next plans to Claude`, so all three wrote one page and two were lost.
Lanyard makes the cart optional and puts the meaningless name in the filename, which is
unique by construction. `2026-10-30-vellum.md` is `vellum`. The cart stays on the page
as a fact, as written. Where a page names a plan in passing ("in pumice"), it uses the
filename's name too.

## Decision: `Chose:` and `Answered:` are kinds, wherever they appear
- where: `src/plan.ts`
- quote: `const match = /^(Decision|Decline|Guess|Changed):\s*(.*)$/.exec(token.text);`

**Because (grommet):** grommet defines them, and vellum's, ferrule's, and tallow's
as-builts use them. Old as-builts keep `Changed:` as written, so `Changed:` stays.

## Decision: An `Answered:` links to the plan's `Guess:` with the same title, and back
- where: `src/render.ts`

**Because (grommet, Claude):** grommet titles an answer with its guess's own title so the
two can be paired. All four answers in mock-control match their guesses exactly. The
rels are `answers` (answer to guess) and `answered-by` (guess to answer), defined in the
profile like the others. An answer with no matching guess says so in plain words.

## Decline: Reading the verdict (held, disproved, not tested) out of an answer's prose
**Because (Claude):** grommet puts it in the prose, and parsing prose for a word is the
kind of guess the HTML shouldn't carry as structure. The answer's text is one link away
from the guess.

## Decline: Checking which kinds belong in a plan and which in an as-built
**Because (Claude):** lanyard: the tool never flags. A `Chose:` in a plan renders as a
`Chose:`.

## Decline: Linking vellum's answer to pumice's guess
- where: `` mock-control: plans/2026-10-30-vellum.as-built.md › Tailwind finds the panel's classes through the `@source` line ``

**Because (Jeff and Claude):** it answers a guess in another plan, which is a link from
one plan into another. That's the cart's question.

## Guess: An exact title match is enough to pair an answer with its guess
**Disproved if:** an `Answered:` title drifts from its guess's title (a typo, a
rephrase) and the page shows a real answer as unpaired.
