# As built: quince

The first phoropter as-built in grommet's kinds. Nothing departed from the plan, so
there's no `Changed:`.

## Chose: A plan page shows its name and its cart as two facts
- where: `src/render.ts`
- quote: `<dt>Name</dt><dd itemprop="name">${esc(plan.name)}</dd>`

**Because (Claude, not asked):** the plan said the cart stays on the page as a fact,
and that's where the name used to be. The name (from the filename) is now the
`name` property. The cart is shown only when the frontmatter has one, exactly as
written. Lists that name a plan in passing (the home page, reverse pages) show the
name, and the reverse page's property for it is now `plan`, not `cart`.

## Chose: How a pair reads
- where: `src/render.ts`
- quote: ``if (entry.answers) return `<p class="pair">Answers ${link('answers', 'answers', entry.answers, 'the plan’s guess')}.</p>`;``

**Because (Claude, not asked):** a line under the entry's heading. "Answers the plan's
guess" on the answer, "Answered in the as-built" on the guess. Two new colors for the
kinds, one for `Chose:` and one for `Answered:`, in both themes.

## Answered: An exact title match is enough to pair an answer with its guess
**Answer (measured):** held on what exists, and not yet tested against what would
disprove it. All four answers in mock-control at `f02528f` pair with their guesses, and
each guess links back. No as-built has an answer whose title drifted, so the case that
would disprove it hasn't happened yet. If one does, the page says "No guess in the plan
has this title," which a reader would notice.
