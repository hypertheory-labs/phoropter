# A note on a landed plan says it matters now, not always that it should change

Tarlatan (drafts/ holds work in progress) called a note on a landed plan a request to
revisit. Jeff, trying it: "Annotating a past plan might just be one of us saying 'hey,
the decision we are making here? We should consider that in the context of this plan'
- so it isn't always a 'we need to change this' type thing." His first two notes, on
dowlas, asked for nothing at all. The mechanism stands: notes kept in `drafts/`, shown
on the plan's page, read back by the TUI. Only the name and the answer change.

## Decision: A note on a landed plan says the plan matters to something now
- where: `guide.md`
- quote: `Notes on a landed plan are a request, and a revisit answers it: a draft whose`

**Because (Jeff):** a note may ask for a revisit, or say "weigh this in what we're
deciding", or just say something. The agent answers in kind: a revisit when the plan's
ground has moved, a citation in the current draft when the plan bears on it (an anchor,
now that the TUI's plans and phoropter's share a repository), or a note in reply. The
guide's section becomes "Notes on a landed plan". The root CLAUDE.md still says to read
them at the start of a session, as notes to weigh, not requests to clear.

## Decision: `requestCount` becomes `noteCount`
- where: `src/render.ts`
- quote: `['requestCount',`

**Because (Claude, not asked):** the profile is a public API, and renaming a property is
a plan, not a tidy-up. This is that plan. `requestCount` is hours old and its one reader,
the TUI, changes in the same commit. The home page says "2 notes" now, not "2 notes
asking for a revisit". In the code, `requestFile` and `readRequest` become
`planNotesFile` and `readPlanNotes`, and the plan's page says the notes are notes.

## Decision: The TUI says "a to note it" on a landed plan
- where: `tui/internal/tui/screens.go`
- quote: `text = "── About this plan · a to ask for a revisit"`

**Because (Jeff):** the key does the same thing it did, so it should say what it does: it
writes a note. Its form says the note goes in `drafts/` and that the plan stays as
written, with nothing about revisiting. A revisit is one thing a note can lead to.
