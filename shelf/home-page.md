# Seed: the home page is getting long

Jeff, 2026-11-19, looking at phoropter's own site: "we'll probably need pagination".
Every plan is listed on the home page, and phoropter has dozens now.

To weigh before paginating: a reader usually wants the recent plans and the open
guesses, not plan 3 of 60, so grouping (this month, then by month) or a short home page
with "all plans" behind a link may serve better than pages 1–N. The list is also what
an agent reads, through the rels and microdata, so one page holding everything has
value there. That's the profile's question, and a change to it is a plan.
