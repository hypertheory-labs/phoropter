# Seed: an exception to an earlier plan, as distinct from overriding it

From Jeff, 2026-11-19, while reading muslin's draft. A plan can run into an earlier one
in two ways:

- **"That plan no longer makes sense for us."** "We planned on webhooks three years ago;
  this overrides that, because…" The guide has this: a revisit, with `revisits:`, asked in
  the old plan's frame.
- **"Just for this, and the earlier plan stays authoritative."** "We're working around
  the earlier plan here, and here's why." The guide has no form for this. It says that
  when work runs into an earlier decision, you don't decide it inside the current plan,
  you stop and revisit. That's right for overriding, and heavy for a deliberate
  exception.

What exists today: a Decision anchored at the earlier entry
(`plans/<file>.md › <entry title>`), whose reason says it's an exception. It shows under
the old plan's "pointed at by". But nothing marks it as an exception rather than a quiet
override, and the guide nearly forbids it.

Jeff's caution, which applies to whatever this becomes: not cop language. Nobody is
"in violation". It's two honest things a plan can say about an earlier one. A format
change, so a plan with the guide changing in the same work.
