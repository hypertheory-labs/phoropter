# Seed: recipes the agent could reach for

Muslin declined serving agent recipes until the first plan shows which ones agents keep
missing. These are candidates, from Jeff's notes on muslin's template (2026-11-19).

- **"Give me a brief."** How this session started: "I've been away, where are we?" Other
  times it's "I haven't been here, but I have a request to do X." Either way: read the
  recent plans and as-builts, notes in `drafts/`, the shelf, and what's still open (a
  guess `not tested`, a choice left to the person), then say where things stand and what
  might come next. Jeff: it could come with a lens, "I made you a lens of what you might
  want to look at", so the brief's references are one key away in the TUI.
- **Ambient plans, noticed.** Some work rests on plans nobody wrote: "keep dependencies
  current", "fix typos". They don't need plans of their own, but the agent can notice
  when one is missing: "I'm about to do the version bump you asked for. No problem. But
  we don't have a plan for keeping this up to date. Should we?" It's an offer, like the
  template's looking back, not a rule.
