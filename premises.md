# Premises

The propositions this project lives in. They aren't claims to prove. They're chosen
ground, kept together because together they add up to more than each one alone (Jeff:
premises in both senses, propositions and a place to live). Changing one is a
conversation, not an edit.

- **Understanding keeps pace with the work.** Coverage, speed and output come second
  to whether the person still knows what the code is for.
- **The proposal comes before the artifact.** A plan is a speculative code review, and
  a polad is a speculative diff. What happened (the as-built, the diff) is read against
  what was proposed. Without the proposal there's nothing to read it against.
- **Review by recall, not recognition.** A diff asks "does this look plausible?", and a
  capable agent's diff always does: LGTM is recognition passing. A plan asks "is this
  what we should do?", and you have to answer that yourself.
- **"Are we writing the right code?" is the person's question.** "Are we writing the
  code right?" is more and more the agent's and the tests'. Attention goes where it
  can't be replaced.
- **A plan outpaces its author's knowledge, and says where.** A Decision says "this,
  because". A Guess says "here, specifically, I might be wrong", with the terms of being
  wrong set in advance. Being exact about doubt is a kind of confidence. Hedging spreads
  doubt so evenly that nobody can find it.
- **Disagreement needs an address.** Declines show the road not taken, Questions ask,
  and Guesses name their disproof. Then challenging means checking a condition, not
  contradicting a person.
- **Plans are testimony; tests are present tense.** The plan holds why, and what was
  turned down, frozen. The test holds that it's still true. A test can be blunt because
  its reason is one step away.
- **Change your mind in a new plan.** What was believed then stays readable.
- **Specimens are ephemeral.** Drafts, lenses, polads and carts gather and propose, then
  get thrown away once something durable holds what mattered. Drift in them is age, not
  a defect.
- **Gathered by hand, not by query.** Someone chose what's in a lens. A smart folder
  whose contents nobody chose gets abandoned.
- **Nothing is a gate.** Silence means proceed. The agent offers, names the other way,
  and has no veto, and wondering isn't asking.
- **No state.** Nothing is open or done, and nothing has an owner or a due date. If it
  feels like a tracker, developers revolt.
- **A name says whom to ask, not who may change it.**
- **A file is reached from a plan.** A file no plan leads to is a finding: nobody wrote
  down why it's that way.
